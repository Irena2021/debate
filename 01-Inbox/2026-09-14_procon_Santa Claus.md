### 议题速览

- 中：这个辩题在争的是：圣诞老人到底是不是真实存在的——他是真实人物、一种精神象征，还是大人编出来骗小孩的谎言。
- EN：This debate asks whether Santa Claus is real—a physical person, a spirit of generosity, or simply a made-up story that adults tell children.
- FR：Ce débat pose la question de savoir si le père Noël existe vraiment — une personne réelle, un symbole de générosité, ou simplement une histoire inventée que les adultes racontent aux enfants.

### 正方立场

- 中：圣诞老人存在，就像爱和慷慨存在一样——看不见摸不着，不代表不存在。
- EN：Santa exists just as surely as love and generosity do—being invisible doesn't mean being unreal.
- FR：Le père Noël existe aussi sûrement que l'amour et la générosité — le fait d'être invisible ne veut pas dire irréel.

- 中：科学和历史数据也支持他存在，比如北美防空司令部追踪他飞行超过五十年。
- EN：Science and historical data back him up too—NORAD has tracked his flight for over 50 years.
- FR：La science et les données historiques le confirment aussi — le NORAD suit son vol depuis plus de 50 ans.

- 中：圣诞老人其实是历史上真实人物圣尼古拉斯的化身，那位主教以慷慨赠礼闻名。
- EN：Santa is really the modern face of St. Nicholas, a real bishop famous for his generosity and gift-giving.
- FR：Le père Noël est en fait l'image moderne de saint Nicolas, un véritable évêque connu pour sa générosité et ses cadeaux.

- 中：他活在全世界人们的心里，所以从精神层面说，他确实「存在」。
- EN：He lives in the hearts of people around the world, so in a spiritual sense, he truly does exist.
- FR：Il vit dans le cœur des gens du monde entier ; au sens spirituel, il existe donc vraiment.

### 反方立场

- 中：世界上苦难太多，一个真正善良的圣诞老人不会允许这些发生。
- EN：There is too much suffering in the world—a truly kind Santa would never allow it.
- FR：Il y a trop de souffrance dans le monde — un père Noël vraiment bon ne le permettrait jamais.

- 中：从科学角度看根本不可能：他要在31小时内访问上亿个家庭，每秒几百次，物理上做不到。
- EN：Science makes it impossible: he would have to visit millions of homes in 31 hours—hundreds per second—which is physically impossible.
- FR：La science le rend impossible : il devrait visiter des millions de foyers en 31 heures — des centaines par seconde — ce qui est physiquement impossible.

- 中：圣诞节本来是庆祝耶稣基督的，圣诞老人不是真相，只是大人集体讲的一个谎言。
- EN：Christmas is really about Jesus Christ; Santa isn't the truth—he's just a lie that adults all agree to tell.
- FR：Noël, c'est avant tout la fête de Jésus-Christ ; le père Noël n'est pas la vérité — c'est juste un mensonge que les adultes acceptent de raconter.

- 中：对孩子说谎是不诚实的，有些家庭坚持「无论如何都要讲真话」。
- EN：Lying to children is dishonest, and some families insist on telling the truth no matter what.
- FR：Mentir aux enfants est malhonnête, et certaines familles tiennent à dire la vérité quoi qu'il arrive.

- EN：He exists as certainly as love and generosity and devotion exist.

- FR：Sert à « introduire un argument » — on met une notion abstraite (l'amour, la générosité) à côté de ce qu'on veut prouver, pour que le nier paraisse absurde.

- EN：Mountains of historical data and more than 50 years of tracking information leads us to believe that…
- 中：用来「引用证据」——用「大量数据 + 多年记录」这种说法增强说服力，适合支持一个看似夸张的观点。
- FR：Sert à « citer des preuves » — l'expression « des montagnes de données + des années de suivi » renforce l'argument, utile pour défendre une idée qui semble exagérée.

- EN：What's Christmas time to you but a time for paying bills without money; a time for finding yourself a year older, but not an hour richer.
- 中：用来「让步转折 / 反问」——用 but 结构反问，先承认对方的说法，再指出它其实很糟糕，语气强烈。
- FR：Sert à « concéder puis retourner l'argument » — la structure avec « but » pose une question rhétorique : on admet l'idée de l'autre pour mieux montrer qu'elle est en fait négative.

## 📖 背景 Background

**English**

Once a year, millions of children around the world eagerly wait for a plump, bearded man dressed in red and white to bring them presents to open on Dec. 25. Known as Santa Claus , his origins are mysterious, and his very existence has been disputed. Some people believe that he lives and works in the North Pole , employs a group of elves to manufacture toys, distributes the gifts annually with the aid of flying reindeer , and regularly utters “Ho, Ho, Ho” in a commanding voice.

**中文**

每年一次，全世界数百万儿童都热切地等待着一个身穿红白衣服、身材圆胖、留着胡子的男人，在12月25日给他们送来礼物。他被称为圣诞老人，他的起源很神秘，他的存在本身也一直有争议。有些人相信他住在北极并在那里工作，雇了一群精灵制造玩具，每年借助会飞的驯鹿分发礼物，还经常用威严的声音说“呵呵呵”。

**Français**

Une fois par an, des millions d’enfants dans le monde attendent avec impatience un homme rond, barbu, vêtu de rouge et de blanc, qui leur apporte des cadeaux à ouvrir le 25 décembre. Connu sous le nom de père Noël, ses origines sont mystérieuses et son existence même a été contestée. Certaines personnes croient qu’il vit et travaille au pôle Nord, emploie une équipe de lutins pour fabriquer des jouets, distribue les cadeaux chaque année avec l’aide de rennes volants et répète régulièrement « Ho, ho, ho » d’une voix autoritaire.

## ✅ 正方论点 Pros

### Pro 1: Santa Claus exists as certainly as love and generosity exist.

**English**

"Yes, Virginia, there is a Santa Claus,” wrote American reporter and publisher Francis Church in his response to a letter to the editor from eight-year-old Virginia O’Hanlon. Published in The New York Sun on Sep. 21, 1897, it is one of the most famous editorials ever written. “He exists as certainly as love and generosity and devotion exist,” explained Church, “and you know that they abound and give to your life its highest beauty and joy.…He lives and he lives forever. A thousand years from now, Virginia, nay, ten times ten thousand years from now, he will continue to make glad the heart of childhood.”

**中文**

正方观点1：圣诞老人确实存在，就像爱与慷慨确实存在一样。

“是的，弗吉尼亚，确实有圣诞老人，”美国记者兼出版人弗朗西斯·丘奇在回应八岁女孩弗吉尼亚·奥汉隆写给编辑的一封信时写道。这篇回应发表于1897年9月21日的《纽约太阳报》，是有史以来最著名的社论之一。“他确实存在，就像爱、慷慨和奉献确实存在一样，”丘奇解释说，“而你知道，这些东西大量存在，并赋予你的生活最高的美和快乐……他活着，而且永远活着。一千年以后，弗吉尼亚，不，十万年以后，他仍会继续让童年之心感到快乐。”

### Pro 2: Science and historical data support the existence of Santa Claus.

**English**

Based on fighter-aircraft photos, we know he has a generous girth (belly), rosy cheeks from sleigh riding in cold weather, and a flowing white beard. “NORAD can confirm that Santa’s sleigh is a versatile, all weather, multipurpose, vertical short-take-off and landing vehicle. It is capable of traveling vast distances without refueling and is deployed, as far as we know, only on December 24th (and sometimes briefly for a test flight about a month before Christmas).” “Science has long shown that Santa Claus is real,” adds neuroscientist Dean Burnett, “and those who claim otherwise are invariably in the pocket of the big toy companies, who don’t want people thinking they can get free playthings and so will pay for their products. The evidence is beyond any reasonable doubt, and the arguments of the Santa deniers have been repeatedly debunked.… “Admittedly, the whole ‘flying reindeer’ thing does seem very far-fetched, and this is a fair accusation. Investigations suggest that the flying reindeer image is a distortion of the truth, in that reindeer are native to the Arctic so Santa may well keep reindeer on his premises and perhaps they did pull his sleigh originally. But there is substantial evidence now to suggest that Santa powers his sled with the energy obtained from a precisely controlled quantum singularity. Basically, Santa has access to a small black hole, which he uses to perform his duties.”

### Pro 3: Santa Claus is the manifestation of the historic St. Nicholas.

**English**

St. Nicholas—Christian bishop of Myra, Turkey, in the 4th century and patron saint of children and sailors—was celebrated in the Eastern and Western churches, noted for his generosity and gift-giving. As Encyclopaedia Britannica reports, St. Nicholas was imprisoned and likely tortured for his faith during the persecution of Christians by the Roman emperor Diocletian but was released under the rule of Constantine the Great. “He was reputed to have given marriage dowries of gold to three girls whom poverty would otherwise have forced into lives of prostitution and to have restored to life three children who had been chopped up by a butcher and put in a tub of brine.” In many countries, children receive gifts on his feast day, Dec. 6. “After the Reformation,” explains Britannica , “devotion to Nicholas disappeared in all the Protestant countries of Europe except Holland, where his legend persisted as Sinterklaas (a Dutch variant of the name St. Nicholas). Dutch colonists took this tradition with them to New Amsterdam (now New York City) in the American colonies in the 17th century. Sinterklaas was adopted by the country’s English-speaking majority under the name Santa Claus….The resulting image of Santa Claus in the United States crystallized in the 19th century, and he has ever since remained the patron of the gift-giving festival of Christmas.”
