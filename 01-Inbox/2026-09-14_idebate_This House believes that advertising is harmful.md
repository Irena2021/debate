## 🎯 三语导读

### 议题速览

- 中：这个辩题在争的是：广告铺天盖地，它到底对社会和人们的生活有害，还是利大于弊。
- EN：This motion asks whether advertising, which is everywhere in modern life, does more harm than good to society and to individuals.
- FR：Ce sujet demande si la publicité, omniprésente dans la vie moderne, fait plus de mal que de bien à la société et aux individus.

### 正方立场

- 中：广告数量太多，已经失控——城市里的人每天可能看到多达五千条广告，一半受访者都觉得广告已经失控。
- EN：There is simply too much advertising, and it is out of control — city dwellers may see up to 5,000 ads a day, and half of those surveyed felt advertising today was out of control.
- FR：Il y a tout simplement trop de publicité, et elle est devenue incontrôlable — les citadins voient jusqu'à 5 000 publicités par jour, et la moitié des personnes interrogées estimaient que la publicité était hors de contrôle.

- 中：广告带来「选择过载」，让人更不快乐——选择越多，人反而越焦虑，尽管物质条件更好，人们却比三十年前更不幸福。
- EN：Advertising creates choice overload, which makes people less happy — the more choices we have, the more stressed we feel, and people are on average less happy than 30 years ago despite being better off.
- FR：La publicité crée une surcharge de choix qui rend les gens moins heureux — plus nous avons de choix, plus nous sommes stressés, et les gens sont en moyenne moins heureux qu'il y a 30 ans malgré une meilleure situation matérielle.

- 中：广告其实是在给人「洗脑」——用醒目的文字、挑逗的图片，甚至伪装成艺术或公益信息，让人防不胜防。
- EN：Advertisements are an attempt to brainwash customers — they use attention-grabbing words, provocative pictures, and even hide inside things that look like art or public information so people don't realise they are being marketed to.
- FR：La publicité cherche à laver le cerveau des consommateurs — elle utilise des mots accrocheurs, des images provocantes, et se cache même dans ce qui ressemble à de l'art ou à de l'information publique pour que les gens ne réalisent pas qu'on leur fait de la promotion.

- 中：广告故意让人因为「没有这个产品」而自卑——尤其扭曲了人们对美和时尚的看法，导致很多年轻人自卑、节食、生活方式不健康。
- EN：Advertisements try to make people feel bad about not having the product — they terribly distort perceptions of beauty and fashion, leading many young people to have low self-esteem and unhealthy lifestyles.
- FR：La publicité cherche à faire sentir aux gens qu'ils sont inférieurs s'ils n'ont pas le produit — elle déforme gravement les perceptions de la beauté et de la mode, ce qui pousse de nombreux jeunes à avoir une mauvaise estime d'eux-mêmes et un mode de vie malsain.

- 中：广告让孩子以为「想要什么就该有什么」——这让人变得物质至上、自私，失去耐心、勤奋、节制等价值观，也伤害了与家人朋友的关系。
- EN：Advertisements tell children that they should have everything they want — this makes people materialistic and selfish, erodes values like patience, hard work and moderation, and harms their relationships with family and friends.
- FR：La publicité fait croire aux enfants qu'ils devraient avoir tout ce qu'ils veulent — cela rend les gens matérialistes et égoïstes, érode des valeurs comme la patience, le travail et la modération, et nuit à leurs relations avec la famille et les amis.

### 反方立场

- 中：小企业需要广告来让产品被人知道——广告其实能拉平竞争，好产品只要营销得当，小公司也能吸引消费者；限制信息反而对大公司有利。
- EN：Small businesses need advertising to make their products known — ads can actually level the playing field, because a good new product marketed cleverly can attract consumers no matter how small the company is; restricting information only helps large companies.
- FR：Les petites entreprises ont besoin de la publicité pour faire connaître leurs produits — la publicité peut en fait égaliser les chances, car un bon nouveau produit bien commercialisé peut intéresser les consommateurs peu importe la taille de l'entreprise ; restreindre l'information ne fait qu'aider les grandes entreprises.

- 中：广告帮我们在不同商品之间做选择——它告诉我们产品的新功能（更强的电脑、能拍照的手机）或更便宜的价格，让我们找到性价比最高的东西。
- EN：Advertising helps us choose between different goods — it draws our attention to products with new features (more powerful computers, phones that are also cameras) or to better prices, helping us find the best value.
- FR：La publicité nous aide à choisir entre différents biens — elle attire notre attention sur des produits aux nouvelles fonctionnalités (ordinateurs plus puissants, téléphones qui sont aussi des appareils photo) ou sur de meilleurs prix, nous aidant à trouver le meilleur rapport qualité-prix.

- 中：广告也在推广健康的产品和生活方式——比如英国曾用名人拍广告推广健康饮食，而香烟等真正有害的广告几乎已经消失。
- EN：Advertisements also promote healthy products and lifestyles — for example, Britain's School Food Trust used celebrities in ads to promote healthy eating, and ads for truly unhealthy products like cigarettes are now almost extinct.
- FR：La publicité fait aussi la promotion de produits et de modes de vie sains — par exemple, le School Food Trust en Grande-Bretagne a utilisé des célébrités dans des publicités pour promouvoir une alimentation saine, et les publicités pour des produits vraiment malsains comme les cigarettes ont presque disparu.

- 中：广告有多烦人，取决于你自己——没人被迫在自己的地盘上放广告，很多公司靠广告收入生存，比如曼联的球衣赞助就值八千万英镑。
- EN：Advertising is only as annoying as you want it to be — no one is forced to put ads on their property, and many organisations depend on ad income, like Manchester United's £80 million shirt sponsorship deal.
- FR：La publicité n'est dérangeante que dans la mesure où on le veut bien — personne n'est obligé de mettre des publicités sur sa propriété, et beaucoup d'organisations dépendent de ces revenus, comme le contrat de sponsoring de maillot de Manchester United d'une valeur de 80 millions de livres.

### 写作借鉴

- EN：Recent research suggests that people are on average less happy than they were 30 years ago — despite being better off and having much more choice.
- 中：用途是「用研究数据支持论点」，先给结论再让步补充，让论证更有说服力。
- FR：Cette phrase sert à appuyer un argument avec des données de recherche, en donnant d'abord la conclusion puis une concession, ce qui rend l'argumentation plus convaincante.

- EN：Advertising has a positive role to play in modern society, helping us choose between competing goods.
- 中：用途是「正面立论」，用 has a positive role to play 直接亮出己方立场，再用 helping 引出具体好处。
- FR：Cette phrase sert à poser un argument positif : on affirme directement le rôle bénéfique de la publicité, puis on précise les avantages concrets avec « helping ».

- EN：No-one is forced to put advertising on their property — for many companies it is an important part of their income.
- 中：用途是「反驳对方」，先否定对方的担忧，再给出反例说明广告其实是收入来源。
- FR：Cette phrase sert à réfuter l'argument adverse : on nie d'abord l'inquiétude soulevée, puis on donne un contre-exemple montrant que la publicité est en fait une source de revenus.

## 📖 背景 Background

**English**

Advertising has grown to be an industry worth many billions of dollars across the world. Online advertising alone is believed to be worth $24 billion a year 1 . Almost all public space has some advertisements in sight and all forms of media, from newspapers to the cinema, are also filled with adverts. Whilst this helps companies sell their products, and helps consumers to learn what is on offer, many believe that this huge amount of advertising can be harmful. It may make people want too much, or things that they cannot have, or it might make them feel inadequate when they don't have something. Research shows that children can be particularly open to these kinds of risk.

Out of control could mean simply that customers think businesses are spending too much on advertising. Without proof that the number of advertisements is having a negative effect, the point is worthless. improve this

### People are given too much choice, which makes them less happy.

**English**

Advertising leads to many people being overwhelmed by the endless need to decide between competing demands on their attention – this is known as the tyranny of choice or choice overload. Recent research suggests that people are on average less happy than they were 30 years ago - despite being better off and having much more choice of things to spend their money on 1 . The claims of adverts crowd in on people, raising expectations about a product and leading to inevitable disappointment after it is bought. A recent advertisement for make-up was banned in Britain due to the company presenting its product as being more effective than it actually was 2 . Shoppers feel that a poor purchase is their fault for not choosing more wisely, and regret not choosing something else instead. Some people are so overwhelmed that they cannot choose at all. 1 Schwartz, The Tyranny of Choice, 2004. 2 Kekeh , Too Beautiful? British MP Draws Line in Sand for Cosmetic Ads , 2011.

**反驳 Counterpoint**：People are unhappy because they can't have everything, not because they are given too much choice and find it stressful. In fact, advertisements play a crucial role in ensuring that what money people have, they spend on the most appropriate product for themselves. If advertisements were not permitted, people would waste money on an initial product when, given the choice, they clearly would go for another. A meta-analysis incorporating research from 50 independent studies found no meaningful connection between choice and anxiety, but speculated that the variance in the studies left open the possibility that choice overload could be tied to certain highly specific and as yet poorly understood pre-conditions 1. 1 ^ Scheibehenne, Benjamin; Greifeneder, R. & Todd, P. M. (2010). "Can There Ever be Too Many Options? A Meta-Analytic Review of Choice Overload" . Journal of Consumer Research 37 : 409-425.

### Advertisements are an attempt to brainwash customers.

**English**

By targeting people's unconscious thoughts adverts are a form of brainwashing that take away people's freedoms to make choices. 1 Anywhere the Eye Can See, It's Likely To See an Ad. The New York Times.

**反驳 Counterpoint**：Adverts which use very sly methods like subliminal images (images which are shown so quickly the viewer doesn't consciously realise they saw them) are already banned. The other forms of advertising are just companies being creative. ### Advertisements tell children that they should have everything they want.

**English**

Advertising gives the impression, especially to children, that they can and should have everything they want. This makes people too interested in material things. People are becoming more selfish and obsessed with their possessions, and losing their values of patience, hard work, moderation and the importance of non-material things like family and friends.
